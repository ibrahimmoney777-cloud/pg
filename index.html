Q: Which file types can I upload? A: PDF and DOCX files up to 25 MB.

Q: How do I contact my supervisor? A: Use the Supervisor Details page from your dashboard.

Q: Why can I not upload? A: The submission deadline may have passed, or archive mode may be enabled.
